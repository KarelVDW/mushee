# Solkey Design System — Working Rules

> The full system (tokens, type specimens, UI kit, brand voice) lives in [`design/README.md`](design/README.md).
> This file is the short rulebook for day-to-day work in `apps/web`. Where the two ever disagree, `design/README.md` wins — and please fix the discrepancy.

**Product North Star: built for the amateur.** Solkey's user is a dabbler — a by-ear player, a hobby songwriter, someone humming a tune — who wants music on paper fast and has never used notation software. Being approachable is our main edge over pro-grade competitors. Every UX call (what ships, button placement, title sizes, motion) answers to that person. The full principles and the _dabbler test_ are in [`design/README.md` → Who we design for](design/README.md#who-we-design-for--the-north-star). The short version:

- **One obvious next step per screen**; secondary actions recede; empty states hand over the button. Never disable the primary action without a visible reason — prefer a default that lets it act.
- **Plain words first** ("Record", "Higher"); a notation term gets a one-line plain hint where a newcomer first meets it. Words follow the [glossary](design/README.md#glossary): recording, score, bar, Sign in / Sign out.
- **Few controls, more on request.** Beginner tools stay visible and advanced ones sit behind a labelled "More". Prefer a good default to a new button.
- **Forgiving:** autosave, undo for everything, and nothing destructive without a confirmation or an undo.
- **Big and touchable:** labels ≥ 11px, touch targets ≥ 40px (44 preferred), WCAG AA, and every icon-only control has a name and a tooltip (§4 Tooltip).
- **Say a limit once, plainly.** Solkey writes melodies, one note at a time; we say so at five set moments ([design/README.md → What Solkey doesn't do](design/README.md#what-solkey-doesnt-do)) — never as a banner, warning or apology.
- **Motion explains, never decorates**; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keyboard + screen reader work everywhere.

**Creative North Star: "The Precision Maverick."** A quiet, white-on-white workspace punctuated by two neon accents. Structured yet rebellious; breathable yet punchy. The workspace stays calm — accents announce action.

## 1. Colors

White-on-white tonal layering plus two accents, each in three intensities:

| Intensity                           | Cyan      | Magenta   | Use for                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | --------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Loud** `*-container`              | `#00DBE9` | `#FF2079` | The action: primary buttons, focus indicators, active-tab underline, the hero CTA's magenta offset shadow. At most one or two per surface.                                  |
| **Quiet** `*-soft`                  | `#A6F2F7` | `#FFADBF` | Identity tags (avatar initials, instrument pills, status chips) and large selectable surfaces (option cards, tap pads, **active** selectable chips). Pair with `on-*-soft`. |
| **Text** bare `primary`/`secondary` | `#00666d` | `#b60052` | Accent/link text, step numbers, destructive text, the input focus stroke. The neons themselves fail WCAG AA as text — never use them for copy.                              |

Note the WCAG consequence: a **selected chip is `secondary-soft`**, not loud magenta — 11px labels on `#FF2079` don't pass contrast. A selected state never relies on colour alone: it pairs the fill with a shape (a check, the 4px stripe, the 3px underline).

- **The No-Line Rule.** No 1px borders for sectioning. Boundaries come from tonal shifts (`surface` → `surface_container_low` → …). The only sanctioned borders: 3px cyan active-tab underline, 2px animated input bottom-stroke, the 15%-opacity ghost border, and the 4px cyan list-row hover stripe.
- **Hover shifts away from the page background.** A white row hovers to `surface_container_high` (#e1e3e3), never to `surface_container_low` (too close to the page's #f6f6f6).
- **Sticky navs** sit on `surface-container-low/85` + `backdrop-blur-xl`.
- **Red is for faults.** `error` / `error_container` mark form errors and real faults only; "can't connect" and offline screens use a neutral `surface_container_high` icon well (`ErrorScreen tone="unreachable"`), and limit or upgrade dialogs stay neutral (§3). The record button and the recording dot are the one non-error use of red.

### The Sanctuary (score canvas)

The editor canvas is strictly `#ffffff` with **`#2d2f2f` ink — never pure black** (`NOTATION_INK` in `packages/notation/src/components/constants.ts`). No cyan, no magenta inside the canvas — with two sanctioned exceptions, both transient by nature and always `data-export-exclude`d: the **live recording waveform** (`RecordingWaveform.tsx`) renders alternating cyan/magenta bars while a take is in flight (silence draws no bars — there is no dotted line between phrases), and the **pitch-operation highlight** (`HighlightLayer.tsx`, `HIGHLIGHT_MAGENTA`) washes the affected range in low-opacity magenta — pulsing under an open transpose popover (a static wash under reduced motion), flashing away when a transpose or "Simplify sharps and flats" lands. All other transient interaction overlays (selection bands, input cursor, playback cursor, the lettered ghost note, the hover tint on an in-score clef/key/time/tempo glyph) use the one sanctioned third color, `INTERACTION_BLUE` (`#1e90ff`) — deliberately neither brand accent, always `data-export-exclude`d so exports stay monochrome. The one exception to the blue: while a take records, the playback cursor turns `RECORDING_CURSOR_RED` (`#ef4444`), the universal recording signal, equally transient and excluded from exports. The optional `manuscript-canvas` dot texture (40px grid, 15% `outline_variant`) may sit behind the staff.

**Chrome on the canvas.** Exactly two pieces of HTML chrome may sit inside the score wrapper. Both render after `<ScoreView>` in DOM order, never inside the SVG (exports read the first `<svg>`), always `data-export-exclude`, in neutral tones — no cyan, no magenta:

- **The editor empty state** (`EditorEmptyState.tsx`), shown while the score has no pitched notes (`Score.hasPitchedNotes`) and nothing plays or records: the title "Sing, hum or play your tune", the one-line how-to from the [monophonic copy table](design/README.md#what-solkey-doesnt-do), a tonal "Start recording" capsule (44px; 40px on phones, so the dock's 54px record stays loudest) and a 13px tertiary line about placing notes by hand. Its inline 8px record-red dot (an `aria-hidden` span, never "●") is its only colour. It is the canvas's accessible description, fades out and moves 8px down over 200ms when the first pitched note lands, and returns whenever the score is all rests again — no stored flag.
- **The bar controls** under the last row's final barline: a tonal "Add bar" chip (12px plus icon; 32px tall, 40px on coarse pointers) and, at least 16px away, an icon-only "Remove last bar" (minus; disabled when one bar is left). Both are real, tab-reachable buttons with tooltips, positioned from the layout's bar-button rect, and hidden during a take. The old SVG `MeasureButton`s stay off.

## 2. Typography

Four families (loaded in `app/layout.tsx`), each with one job:

- **Space Grotesk** — display + UI: wordmark, headlines, buttons, labels. The wordmark is `Solkey`, 700 italic, tracked `-0.04em`.
- **Manrope** — body copy at reading sizes.
- **Newsreader italic** — the warm serif clause in display headlines, **marketing + auth surfaces only**. In-app chrome (Library, Editor, Settings — including the score-title field) stays Space Grotesk.
- **Geist Mono** — numeric readouts: bpm, prices, step numbers, time signatures, the recording clock, the 6-digit code field.

ALL-CAPS only for `Eyebrow`-style schematic labels (11px, `0.12em` tracking). Everything else is sentence case. No label anywhere is smaller than 11px (`Badge` included).

## 3. Depth & surfaces

- **Cards:** `surface_container_lowest` + `tonal-layer-glow` (24px blur, 6% `on_surface`). List rows and compact cards use `rounded-md`; standalone cards and panels `rounded-lg`; full sheets `rounded-xl`.
- **Dialogs** are the one glass surface: `.glass-panel` (85% white + 12px blur) + `editorial-shadow` (19px tier) + `rounded-lg`, over the scrim `bg-on-surface/40 backdrop-blur-xs`. `DialogPanel` renders its own scrim and already carries the a11y contract (role, focus trap, Escape, focus restore, one `onClose` for Escape/backdrop/×, its subtitle linked as the description) — always build dialogs from it.
- **Popovers, menus, tooltips and sheets are opaque:** `surface_container_lowest` + `editorial-shadow`. Blur doesn't nest — a glass panel opened inside the backdrop-filtered header or dock would sample only that bar and show the staff through its text. (The header and dock keep their own blur on an `aria-hidden` backdrop layer, so anything opened from them still sits over the real page.) Anchored popovers build from `Popover`/`PopoverOption` (dialog role, Escape + outside-click dismissal, no editor shortcut fires behind an open one); on phones the same `Popover` renders as a sheet (§4b).
- **A popover may carry one plain hint line** (`Popover hint`, 13px Manrope `on_surface_variant`) under its eyebrow — required for every popover named by a notation term: clef ("Treble for higher notes, bass for lower ones."), time, key, tempo, transpose. Option grids lead with the two to four choices a newcomer needs and put the rest behind a labelled More ("More clefs", "More time signatures", Transpose's "More options"). The clef picker leads with Treble and Bass plus the instrument's usual clef (`Instrument.defaultClef` — a viola's Alto, "Usual for viola") and the clef in use, each listed once.
- **Limit and upgrade dialogs stay calm.** The score-limit, daily-recording-limit and change-plan dialogs use the neutral tone — never `error_container`, no alarm callout — say that the user's work is safe, and end in one flat PrimaryButton (no pop). A button that leads to payment says so and names the price.
- **Never black shadows.** Always `on_surface` at 6%.
- **The magenta pop** (`emphasis="pop"`: 3px→5px offset shadow + −2px hover lift) is reserved for **at most one hero CTA per viewport** — landing hero and final CTA, the top-nav "New score". Dialog confirms (Change plan included), section actions, and utility buttons stay flat.

## 4. Components

- **Buttons:** Primary = flat loud-cyan capsule; Secondary = tonal capsule; Tertiary = bare text. Destructive = `danger` red fill (never gets the pop). All interactive elements share the focus ring: the `focus-ring` utility (`packages/ui/src/theme.css` — 2px `primary` outline, 2px offset, on `:focus-visible`).
- **Inputs:** `surface_container_low` fill, `rounded-sm`, a 2px **`primary`** (#00666d) bottom stroke animating in from the centre on focus — the neon cyan fails 3:1 non-text contrast on the input fill. No four-sided boxes. Text renders at 14px on fine pointers and **16px on coarse pointers** (iOS zooms the page below 16px); never disable pinch zoom. Every auth field carries `autoComplete` (and `inputMode` where it helps: the numeric 6-digit code, the bpm field); no fake-mask placeholders. The placeholder (`on_surface_variant`) is never the only label — label-less fields such as search take an `ariaLabel` ("Search your scores", "Search instruments"). An error sits under its field, linked by `aria-invalid` + `aria-describedby`.
- **Tooltip** (`src/components/ui/Tooltip.tsx`) — every icon-only control in the editor chrome, the dock and the app wears one, and so does any control whose label alone doesn't say what it does. Text pattern **"Name — hint · key"**: the name, an optional one-line plain hint, and the live shortcut (from the editor's Keybindings, so a rebound key shows; ⌘ on Apple, Ctrl elsewhere; fine pointers only; left out when unbound). It opens after 400ms of hover, or at once on keyboard `:focus-visible` (not on focus restored by a closing popover), sits 8px above dock controls and 8px below header controls, and is an opaque 13px card. A disabled control's tooltip says why ("Select a note first"). Never on touch hover: on coarse pointers a 450ms press-and-hold shows the name in a bubble, releasing still fires the control and sliding off cancels both. The trigger's `aria-label` stays its accessible name.
- **Disabled controls:** disable only what truly can't act, and say why — the tooltip on desktop, an inline hint or caption on touch. Prefer `aria-disabled` (still focusable, still explains) to a dead native `disabled`. Record is never disabled for lack of a selection.
- **Triggers announce what they do:** a control that opens a popover or menu exposes `aria-haspopup` + `aria-expanded` (`ChipToggle popup`, `HeaderMenu`); a real on/off toggle uses `aria-pressed`. A control that swaps its action names the action it will do next (Record ↔ "Stop recording", Play ↔ Pause).
- **Toasts** (`@mushee/ui` `Toaster`): two always-mounted regions — info `role="status"`, errors `role="alert"`; at most two toasts visible; info lasts 6s, errors scale with their length (at least 8s) or `persist` when they ask the user to act; every timer pauses on hover, focus or touch. Up to two action buttons (`action`, or `actions` for two — 40px text buttons in reading order, wrapping under the message below `sm`; each with its own `ariaLabel` when its label would clash — "Undo this recording" vs the header's Undo). **In the editor toasts stack above the dock** (the page sets `--toast-bottom` from the dock's height) and never cover the transport; during a take, news goes to the recording strip, not to toasts. **Big edits offer Undo in a toast** — removing a bar that held notes, a multi-note delete, transpose, Simplify sharps and flats, a meter change that regroups bars ("Moved 2 half steps higher." · Undo). Single-note edits never toast; the toast's Undo works only while its step is still the latest, and the toast leaves when another edit lands.
- **FAQ** (`FaqList`): a native `<details>` disclosure list — summary ≥ 44px at 17px, answer 16px Manrope, the first item open, the chevron rotating over 150ms (none under reduced motion). The first question everywhere is "Can Solkey write chords?" (see [design/README.md → What Solkey doesn't do](design/README.md#what-solkey-doesnt-do)).
- **List rows** open on a click anywhere in the row. Secondary row actions live in one "More" popover (a 40px `more-horizontal` icon button, "More actions for {title}": Rename, Duplicate, Delete), never as a strip of icon buttons.
- **Instrument pickers** (`InstrumentPicker`, shared by New score, Import, Change instrument and onboarding's tiles) show eight popular instruments first (`Instrument.popular()`, ≥ 48px tiles) and the full grouped list behind "All instruments"; typing in the search opens the full list. A chordal pick (`instrument.playsChords`) carries the one-line melody hint from the monophonic copy table — 13px `on_surface_variant` text, no icon, no fill. Change instrument's flat confirm names the pick ("Use Violin") and waits, saying why, until a different instrument is picked.
- **Upgrade moments deep-link** to the exact place to act — `/settings?tab=plan&plan={next tier}` (Change plan opens on that tier; `PlanCatalogue.nextAfter`), `&packs=1` for minute packs — never a generic page. Settings has four tabs, each addressable by `?tab=`: Profile (the bare `/settings`), Plan, Recordings, Account. The avatar is the account menu (name, email, Settings, **Sign out**).
- **Shared score page** (`/s/[token]`): the transport is a labelled player bar above the score (a 44px "Play" ⇄ "Pause", "Stop" once playback started, a "Download" menu, "Save a copy"), never header icons; the header holds the wordmark and one CTA.
- **The editor header** (slim, the chrome mirror of the dock): back · score title (ellipsis at rest, full text on focus, a "Rename" tooltip, "Untitled score" when empty) · instrument chip ("Change instrument") · **save status** — on desktop a quiet "Saving…" then "Saved" with a check that shrinks to the check alone; at any size a failed save reads "Not saved yet — retrying" in error text until it recovers, and tapping it retries now. The transport sits centred. The right end holds the labelled **Transpose** chip, Undo / Redo, the keyboard-shortcuts chip (its tooltip names "?", the key that opens the list), then the score-level chips **Share** (read-only link), **Recordings** (recorded audio) and **Export** — each a `HeaderMenu`: a `ChipToggle` in popup mode opening a downward opaque `Popover` (`role="dialog"`, Escape + outside-click dismissal, focus moved in and handed back to the chip), never a dock item. The tab reads "{title} — Solkey" ("Untitled score — Solkey" until the score loads or while it has no title, "Score not found — Solkey" for a missing one) — `useDocumentTitle`, which keeps a client-known title on top of a late-streamed metadata title.
- **The transport** (`TransportControls`): Record is the largest control on desktop too (48px vs Play 40px). Stop keeps its slot but shows only while something plays or records. Every transport button wears a tooltip with its live key ("Record a melody · ⇧R", "Hear it back · Space", "Stop recording · Space"). The metronome wears the `metronome` glyph; its on state is a `primary_container` fill plus `aria-pressed`, and it is described "Clicks during playback. Recording always counts you in." (on phones: the More sheet's Metronome switch, hint "A steady click while playing and recording."). While a take runs, Record turns into Stop: a white stop square and the name "Stop recording". **Record is never disabled for lack of a selection** — with nothing selected, a take starts after the last bar that holds notes (bar 1 on an empty score). Space plays, pauses and stops a take; ⇧Space plays from the start; ⇧R records; Escape stops a take. **During a take the score is view-only** — taps on notes and glyphs are ignored, and only Stop (or Space / Escape) ends a take. After a take, the selection moves quietly to the bar after it, and a summary toast above the dock ("Added bars 3–6. Click any note to fix it.") offers "Hear it back" and "Undo" ("Undo this recording" — the whole recording, in one step); it goes with the next edit, the next recording or the editor.
- **The Tool-Dock:** the editor's note tools live in a docked bar along the editor's bottom edge — the chrome mirror of the slim header (`surface-container-low/85` + `backdrop-blur-xl`, on an `aria-hidden` backdrop layer), never floating over the score. The canvas scrolls edge-to-edge between the two bars. Each tool group sits in a **white capsule well** (`surface_container_lowest` + tonal glow — the card lift, shared by the `Segmented` track and `ToolGroup`): the well is what says "buttons live here" and where one group ends, so groups need only 1.5rem of space between them — **no dividers**. Controls inside a well are transparent at rest (`plain`); controls standing alone on the chrome (header chips, transport circles) carry the white fill themselves. Popovers open upward, away from the dock.
    - **Desktop order**, left to right: **[Pitch: Higher · Lower]** — the mouse twin of ↑ ↓ — **[Length:** whole … sixteenth **+ Dotted]** in one well (Dotted is a drawn dotted-note glyph, never a "·") — **[♭ ♮ ♯]** — **[Rest]** — **[More ▴]** — **[Clef · key · time · tempo]**. Triplet, Tie and Simplify sharps and flats live behind the labelled "More" chip, in the "More note tools" popover (44px rows, each with its plain hint and key). New tool groups wrap onto additional rows (desktop; on phones new tools go into More).
    - **Score chips show words:** "D major" / "A minor" (phones "D" / "Am"), "4/4", "100 bpm" (lowercase unit). With nothing selected they show bar 1's real values, stay enabled and apply at bar 1. Note tools with nothing selected are `aria-disabled` with the tooltip "Select a note first"; a run of notes shows a mixed state (nothing pressed unless every note shares it).
    - **One hint line** (contract C10): a single 13px line along the dock's top edge, one hint at a time, fading in over 200ms — today the first-selection hint ("To move a note, click higher or lower on the staff, or press ↑ ↓."), shown once per viewer until the first pitch edit.
    - **The recording strip** replaces the tool wells for the length of a take: one white capsule well, 56px tall, centred (`role="group"` "Recording status"). The count-in reads "Get ready" over one dot per felt pulse (4 in 4/4, 2 in 6/8), each filling red on its click; then an 8px red dot with a slow pulse, "Recording" and the elapsed clock. Below, one hint line: how to stop ("Press Space or ■ to stop"), the time left today on a capped plan with a minute or less to go, or — during the count-in, once per browser session — the iPhone Mic Mode reminder. When transcription drops out, a warning state takes the line ("Solkey can't write notes right now. Your audio is still being saved, and this time won't count.").
- **Selection actions live in the selection popover, never the dock** — on both layouts. Desktop opens it on right-click, on releasing a mouse drag that extended the selection, and on the ContextMenu key / Shift+F10; phones on long-press, double-tap, lifting a range drag, or a tap on the already-selected note. The floating bar (`SelectionPopover` — deliberately OS-flavored, mirroring Android's text-selection menu) holds copy / paste / delete / select all and stays on screen after Select all. A destructive action from it raises an Undo toast. Selection-scoped actions are never added as dedicated dock or action-row buttons.
- **Screen readers:** the editor owns one polite live region (`EditorAnnouncer`, fed through `manipulator.announce()`, debounced 150ms). Every selection, edit and recording state change announces in plain words — "G, quarter note. Bar 2, beat 1.", "5 notes selected, bars 1 to 2.", "Recording", "Recording stopped. Added bars 3 to 6." The score container is `role="application"` with the role description "score editor", named "{title} — score" and described by the live arrow-key shortcuts, ending "Press ? for all shortcuts." — "?" (fixed, matched by the character on any layout, desktop only) opens the shortcuts list.
- **Motion:** `--ease-solkey` (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`); 150ms for color hovers, 200ms for shadow/transform, 300ms for tab/stroke sweeps. No bounces, no fade-up-on-scroll. Every animation has a reduced-motion form (the global rule in `packages/ui/src/theme.css`; see design/README.md → Animation).

## 4b. Mobile (< 768px)

Phones get the same design language, restructured — never a shrunken desktop:

- **The score reflows, it never scales down.** `ScoreLayout` packs rows against the container width (`Score.setLayoutWidth`, floor 340 units), so notation keeps full glyph size and the score reads as a vertically scrolling column of 1–2-bar rows. Exports stay print-shaped: PDF pins the layout back to the standard 1000-unit width for the snapshot.
- **Transport moves into the dock; the dock is two rows.** On phones the header keeps only back / title / instrument chip and the three icon-only header chips (Share / Recordings / Export); their panels stay 16px inside the viewport, whichever chip they hang from. The dock is a **tool row** — Undo, the note lengths and a labelled "More" tile — over the **action row**: a "Note" well (◀ ▶, select previous / next note), a "Pitch" well (▼ ▲, named "Lower" / "Higher" like the desktop dock's nudges), then stop / play / **record**. Each well is labelled in 11px under its buttons. The record button is always the largest control on screen (54px vs 46px play, 40px for the rest — 44px from 400px wide); its emphasis never disappears.
- **Everything else lives in the "More tools" sheet**, each item with a text label and its current value: "This note" (Dotted, Triplet, Rest, Tie, Sharp or flat — each captioned in plain words) and "Whole score" (Clef, Key, Time, Tempo, Transpose, Simplify sharps and flats, Metronome, Redo). Tapping a "Whole score" row swaps the sheet body to that picker with a "‹ Back" link — sheets never stack.
- **During a take** the tool and action rows collapse (200ms height transition, none under reduced motion) to the recording strip beside the 54px record-as-stop; the separate Stop hides until the take ends, so the score gets the room.
- **Touch replaces hover and the keyboard.** Tap selects; horizontal drag extends the selection (`touch-action: pan-y` keeps vertical scrolling native); the hover ghost-pitch preview is mouse-only — pitch edits go through the Pitch nudges. Press-and-hold on an icon-only control shows its name (the touch tooltip) without blocking it; long-press on the score canvas still opens the selection popover. The keyboard-shortcuts entry point is hidden. The selection and the playback/recording cursor auto-scroll their row into view. The first time a note is selected on a phone, a one-time toast says "Tap the note again to copy or delete it."
- **Dock popovers become sheets.** Every phone sheet — dock chips, More rows, in-score glyph taps (`COMPACT_POPOVER_SHEET`) — renders through the one `PopoverSheetHost` at the editor root, outside the header's and dock's blur, in a frame between the measured header and dock (`--app-header-h`, `--dock-h`; never `vh`). Sheets are opaque white (no glass), never taller than that frame (the body scrolls, with a fade at the clipped edge), and carry a title, a one-line hint and a 40px "Close"; they rise and fade in over 200ms and show a pick for 150ms before closing. On desktop those in-score popovers anchor at the clicked glyph, clamped inside the score wrapper (`AttributePopoverAnchor` in `src/app/scores/[id]/ScoreAttributePopover.tsx`).
- **Toasts never cover the action row.** On the phone editor they stack above the dock; during a take recording news goes to the strip instead.
- **Tap targets grow on coarse pointers.** The shared controls carry the `pointer-coarse:` bumps — don't hand-size touch targets per screen: primary and secondary buttons ≥ 44px; tertiary buttons, text links, icon buttons (the password reveal and every × included), `Chip` (`packages/ui` `Inputs.tsx`), `ChipToggle` and `Segmented` options ≥ 40px; `TransportBtn` ≥ 40px for every transport and navigation circle (record stays largest at 54px); nav and footer links ≥ 40px.
- **Text inputs are 16px on coarse pointers** (iOS focus zoom); never disable pinch zoom.
- **Full-height surfaces use `dvh`, never `100vh`** (mobile browser toolbars), and the dock pads `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.
- **Page scaffolding stacks below `md`:** multi-column grids go single-column, the auth card stacks its brand panel into a strip, settings' side rail becomes a tab row (four labels, no icons), the library table collapses to title + updated cards with no visible column header — Import becomes the tertiary "Import a file", and search appears from 6 scores. The cookie banner is a compact bottom sheet with the page padded underneath, so it never covers a page's primary CTA. Section padding steps down (`px-5 sm:px-8`, `py-14 sm:py-22`); display type steps down one size (`text-[34px] sm:text-[48px]`, hero `text-[40px] sm:text-[56px] lg:text-[72px]`).

## 5. Do / Don't

**Do**

- Define regions with tonal shifts; lift hovers away from the page tone.
- Keep one loud accent per moment — if two things shout, neither is heard.
- Reference tokens (`var(--color-…)`, `--shadow-*`) — never re-hardcode a hex the palette already names.
- Show limits before they bite ("3 of 5 free scores"), and say what still works when one is reached.
- Give every icon-only control a name and a tooltip, and every disabled control its reason.

**Don't**

- No divider lines between list items — use background shifts.
- No gradients on buttons or CTAs; the energy is the offset shadow.
- No color in the score canvas beyond ink + `INTERACTION_BLUE` overlays and the transient exceptions in §1.
- Never preselect a paid option in a flow entered through a free CTA; a button that leads to payment says so, price in the label ("Continue to payment · $9/month").
- Never disable the primary action without a visible reason — prefer a default that lets it act.
- No "take" in UI copy — it's a "recording" (and a "bar", never a "measure"; see the [glossary](design/README.md#glossary)).
- No glass behind text: menus, popovers, tooltips, sheets and the cookie / browser-support banners are opaque.
- No toast over the transport or the dock.
- No emoji, no icon fonts, no unicode glyphs as chrome icons (`♮ ♭ ♯` in the tool dock's accidental control are the only exception — they're notation — and they never stand in as icons for any other action).
