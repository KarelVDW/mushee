## How to review

`git log --first-parent 940f307..polish-2026-10` lists one merge commit per package
(`merge(phase1): <package>`), then the review-fix merges (`merge(review): …`). Each package's commits
inside its merge are small and cite the audit step ids they implement (e.g. `[web-editor-M07]`).

## How Phase 1 was produced

1. **Audit:** 16 areas, each read in full by a structure-level auditor and then a function-level one.
   Every finding went to a value judge and a fact judge, and a finding only survived if neither
   rejected it. That gave 418 steps.
2. **Integration:** one integrator deduplicated concepts that several areas had proposed. For example,
   `ScoreLayout.pointAtBeat` was proposed five times; 111 concepts ended up with exactly one owner.
   It also scope-trimmed admin and eval to small cleanups (`apps/api/scripts` was out of scope), and
   ordered the work into 29 packages over 6 waves, with no two packages in a wave sharing a file.
3. **Implementation:** each package was implemented in its own worktree, reviewed adversarially, fixed,
   then merged. Every wave passed the full gate.
4. **Close-out:** a deferred sweep of 28 items. Then a whole-diff review by 7 reviewers (6 by area plus
   one cross-cutting), with a skeptic for each high or medium finding, fixes grouped by area, a review
   of those fixes, and the final gate.

## Deliberate behaviour changes (verified fixes, not pure refactoring)

- **Editor:**
    - Tie is enabled only where the note can tie (this fixed the two September e2e failures).
    - Dotted is disabled with nothing selected.
    - Toggle-rest puts the note on the clef's middle line.
    - Transpose "selection" scope needs more than one selected note.
    - A range selection survives clef, key and tempo edits from the dock.
    - Record can stop a take, and is disabled with no selection.
    - Backspace in a popover no longer navigates back in history (webkit).
- **Editor glue:**
    - The share toggle no longer re-attaches the load-time document.
    - The history lock lasts the whole take and is released on socket drain.
    - Autosave accumulates title and score changes into one PATCH (this fixes the title-loss race).
    - A raw (non-JSON) stored document shows an error screen instead of a silent blank (now tested).
- **Playback:** editing tempo or meter mid-playback re-anchors the remaining schedule instead of
  replaying a burst. This was a regression from the one-clock change; the final review caught it and
  it is fixed.
- **Notation:**
    - `setInstrument` re-keys, folds and respells.
    - Tuplets respect beams.
    - Dotted-note widths are measured correctly.
    - Multi-digit time signatures engrave correctly.
    - The hover-ghost condition is fixed.
- **API:**
    - Pack drawdown read TypeORM's `[rows, count]` UPDATE shape wrongly, so `drawPackSeconds` always
      returned 0. Fixed.
    - Edit-cache patches had the same problem, plus a lost-edit fix.
    - Recording WebSocket frames are validated (DoS hardening) and score/recording ids are UUID-checked.
    - Deleting a score deletes its takes' audio.
    - The account purge relies on the FK cascades.
    - The loudnorm default flipped to false; every production and eval decode passes it explicitly, so
      nothing changes.
- **Web:**
    - Body-less POSTs no longer send `'{}'`.
    - The proxy matcher skips any path with a dot (PWA and apple-touch icons are no longer gated).
    - Signed-in Sketch-card clicks no longer land on a cancel-preselected plan dialog.
    - "New score" from other pages opens the library's dialog (`/scores?new=1`).
    - Change plan can open with a tier preselected.
    - Bulgaria is in the eurozone.
    - The shared page no longer loads metronome samples.

## Follow-ups

- **webkit `shared.spec.ts:8` flake** (pre-existing): Next streams `generateMetadata`'s fallback
  `<title>`, which can land after `SharedScorePage`'s `document.title` effect. Fix in the page (set the
  title from metadata only) or make the test wait for the final title.
- **Admin proxy:** the route comment says `encodeURIComponent` keeps `..` segments inert, but
  `encodeURIComponent('..') === '..'`, and fetch normalizes the URL, so `/api/admin/../x` could reach a
  non-`/admin` API route carrying the admin secret header. Only someone already signed in to the console
  can reach it, so the risk is low; still, reject `.`/`..` segments in the proxy.
- **API, optional:** make `describeError` API-wide (4 inline copies remain). Adopting the now-public
  pipeline settings in `apps/api/scripts` was left out on purpose, because it would move eval numbers.
- **README quickstart:** `cp apps/api/.env.example apps/api/.env.development` overwrites an existing
  file; use `cp -n`.
- About 50 low review notes were not fixed because they weren't straightforward. They're in the
  session's review report; none affects behaviour.
- **Inputs for Phase 2/3**, found by the screenshot harness before the refactor and still open:
    - The phone dock sheets (key, transpose) run under the header.
    - The phone share popover spills off the left edge.
    - "Select all" on phone hides the selection popover under the header.
    - The signed-in shared-page header overflows on phone.
    - The editor title field is far too narrow.
    - The tempo chip shows 90 during a take of a score marked 108.
    - The cookie banner is translucent.
    - "Studio · Free" appears for an account with no Polar subscription.
    - There's no "5 of 5 scores" indicator.
    - On phone, toasts land over the record button.
    - The transpose highlight has no base opacity.
    - The plan dialog's CTA carries the magenta pop shadow.
    - The mic-mode guide is titled "iPhone user".
    - The onboarding code error is capped at 280px.
    - Plus the monophonic messaging (new users don't realise recording writes one note at a time).
