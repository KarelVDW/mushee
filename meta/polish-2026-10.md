## How to review

`git log --first-parent 940f307..polish-2026-10` lists one merge commit per package
(`merge(phase1): <package>`), then the review-fix merges (`merge(review): …`). Each package's commits
inside its merge are small and cite the audit step ids they implement (e.g. `[web-editor-M07]`).

## How Phase 1 was produced

1. **Audit:** 16 areas, each read in full by a structure-level auditor and then a function-level one.
   Every finding went to a value judge and a fact judge, and a finding only survived if neither
   rejected it. That gave 418 steps.
2. **Integration:** one integrator deduplicated concepts that several areas had proposed. For example,
   `ScoreLayout.pointAtBeat` was proposed five times; 111 concepts ended up with exactly one owner.
   It also scope-trimmed admin and eval to small cleanups (`apps/api/scripts` was out of scope), and
   ordered the work into 29 packages over 6 waves, with no two packages in a wave sharing a file.
3. **Implementation:** each package was implemented in its own worktree, reviewed adversarially, fixed,
   then merged. Every wave passed the full gate.
4. **Close-out:** a deferred sweep of 28 items. Then a whole-diff review by 7 reviewers (6 by area plus
   one cross-cutting), with a skeptic for each high or medium finding, fixes grouped by area, a review
   of those fixes, and the final gate.

## Deliberate behaviour changes (verified fixes, not pure refactoring)

- **Editor:**
    - Tie is enabled only where the note can tie (this fixed the two September e2e failures).
    - Dotted is disabled with nothing selected.
    - Toggle-rest puts the note on the clef's middle line.
    - Transpose "selection" scope needs more than one selected note.
    - A range selection survives clef, key and tempo edits from the dock.
    - Record can stop a take, and is disabled with no selection.
    - Backspace in a popover no longer navigates back in history (webkit).
- **Editor glue:**
    - The share toggle no longer re-attaches the load-time document.
    - The history lock lasts the whole take and is released on socket drain.
    - Autosave accumulates title and score changes into one PATCH (this fixes the title-loss race).
    - A raw (non-JSON) stored document shows an error screen instead of a silent blank (now tested).
- **Playback:** editing tempo or meter mid-playback re-anchors the remaining schedule instead of
  replaying a burst. This was a regression from the one-clock change; the final review caught it and
  it is fixed.
- **Notation:**
    - `setInstrument` re-keys, folds and respells.
    - Tuplets respect beams.
    - Dotted-note widths are measured correctly.
    - Multi-digit time signatures engrave correctly.
    - The hover-ghost condition is fixed.
- **API:**
    - Pack drawdown read TypeORM's `[rows, count]` UPDATE shape wrongly, so `drawPackSeconds` always
      returned 0. Fixed.
    - Edit-cache patches had the same problem, plus a lost-edit fix.
    - Recording WebSocket frames are validated (DoS hardening) and score/recording ids are UUID-checked.
    - Deleting a score deletes its takes' audio.
    - The account purge relies on the FK cascades.
    - The loudnorm default flipped to false; every production and eval decode passes it explicitly, so
      nothing changes.
- **Web:**
    - Body-less POSTs no longer send `'{}'`.
    - The proxy matcher skips any path with a dot (PWA and apple-touch icons are no longer gated).
    - Signed-in Sketch-card clicks no longer land on a cancel-preselected plan dialog.
    - "New score" from other pages opens the library's dialog (`/scores?new=1`).
    - Change plan can open with a tier preselected.
    - Bulgaria is in the eurozone.
    - The shared page no longer loads metronome samples.

## Phase 2 — UX, amateur-first

**Brief:** the target user is an amateur or dabbler, not a professional. The app should get their music on
paper quickly, without technical mumbo-jumbo or complicated button layouts. It should also be clear
along the way, subtly but clearly, that recording is monophonic.

**How it was produced:**

1. Docs first. `apps/web/design/README.md` → "Who we design for" (8 principles + the dabbler test) is now
   the yardstick.
2. Audit. 7 surface auditors (first-run, desktop editor, phone editor, recording, library/account, public,
   copy and a11y) worked from the real screenshots and the code. A dabbler-advocate judge and a
   reality/design-system judge then checked every finding. 133 findings were kept; the judges sharpened
   rather than rejected them.
3. Integration. One integrator turned the findings into 12 packages with shared contracts that every
   package honours: C1 toasts, C2 tooltips, C3 phone sheets, C12 glossary, C13 monophonic copy, and others.
4. Implementation in 6 two-package batches. Each package went through implement → review → fix, and each
   batch passed a full gate.
5. Three follow-up packages for deferred and declined items.
6. Close-out. A full gate including webkit, a fresh screenshot set, and a UX acceptance review by 4
   lenses with a skeptic per finding. It confirmed 3 high, 19 medium and 26 low findings, all fixed and
   re-reviewed. Then a final gate.

**Monophonic messaging (your decision):** five touchpoints, each in plain words — "melodies, one note at
a time":

- landing How it works 02;
- the first FAQ question, "Can Solkey write chords?", on the landing page and on /pricing;
- the hint when you pick a chordal instrument;
- the empty score;
- the import notice when chords were reduced to one note.

It does not appear at the count-in, in toasts or tooltips, or on the onboarding mic step.

**What changed for a newcomer** (from the acceptance reviews):

- **First run:**
    - Before, onboarding was 8 steps, raw "Invalid OTP", an empty library and a silent empty score.
    - Now it is: verify (the code submits itself) → "What will you record?" → microphone → one optional
      survey → done → "Record my first melody".
    - That last step opens a ready score with a guiding card, and focus lands on "Start recording".
- **Editor:**
    - The dock reads as words ("Higher/Lower", "Rest", "C major", "100 bpm"), with a labelled "More" for
      everything else.
    - Tooltips are "Name — hint · key", and on touch they show on press-and-hold.
    - The phone dock went from four glyph rows to one row plus labelled wells. Picker sheets sit above the
      dock.
    - Transpose speaks in half steps with previews like "now in D major".
    - There is a save status and Undo toasts. An editor announcer gives screen readers live messages. "?"
      opens the shortcuts.
- **Recording:**
    - During a take, a recording strip replaces the dock: "Get ready" with one dot per pulse, then the clock.
    - Record turns into "Stop recording".
    - After a take, a toast says "Added bars 1–4. Click any note to fix it." with "Hear it back" and "Undo".
    - Record continues after the music instead of in front of it. The first note is no longer selected on
      load.
    - The daily budget is in human units ("2 h 59 min left", "Refills tomorrow at 2:00 AM"). The API now
      sends `resetsAt`.
    - Limit dialogs open before the mic does.
- **Library and settings:**
    - A whole row opens its score, with the other actions in one menu.
    - The count line shows when you're capped, and the limit dialog opens before the form.
    - New score takes an optional title, with popular instruments first.
    - Settings has Profile / Plan / Recordings / Account tabs, and deep links reach each tab.
    - The plan card and packs dialog are in minutes.
- **Glossary everywhere:**
    - "recording", not "take"; "bar", not "measure"; "Simplify sharps and flats".
    - Sentence case.
    - The offline screens match.
- **Public pages:**
    - The landing hero is for signed-out visitors, and the FAQ sits on both landing and pricing.
    - Public pages have a skip link.
    - The cookie banner is opaque.
    - The shared page header fits on phones.
- **Accessibility:**
    - 40px coarse-pointer floors on the shared controls.
    - `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected everywhere.
    - Live announcements for state changes.
    - Toasts use role=status/alert.
    - Icon-only controls have names and tooltips.
- **Docs:** `apps/web/design/README.md` and `apps/web/DESIGN.md` describe the new UX, including how
  monophonic recording is communicated.

## Phase 3 — aesthetics

**Brief:**

- Keep the colour scheme and the modern, playful, youthful character.
- Fix the inconsistencies and rough edges.
- Make the features added after the original design direction feel native.
- Reach top-tier, go-live attractiveness that keeps new users "magnetized".

**How it was produced:**

1. **Audit.** Seven visual auditors (marketing, auth and onboarding, library and settings, desktop editor,
   phone editor, recording and feedback, and the design system itself) worked from the post-UX
   screenshots and the code. A top-tier-designer judge and a design-system/feasibility judge checked
   each finding: 127 kept, 1 rejected.
2. **Integration.** One integrator merged them into 8 packages in 4 two-package batches, with system and
   token changes first. It was capped at 8 packages for the weekly budget; what didn't fit is on the
   _later_ list below.
3. **Implementation.** Each package went through implement → review → fix, and each batch passed a gate.
4. **Close-out.** A full gate including webkit, a fresh screenshot set, and visual QA by 2 lenses with a
   skeptic per finding. QA found 1 high and 11 medium findings, all fixed and reviewed, then a second
   gate.

**Rule change, approved by Karel on 2026-10-23: glass is retired.**

- Dialogs, menus, popovers, tooltips, sheets, toasts, the selection bar and the banners are opaque white
  with the new overlay shadow tier.
- On desktop, dialogs pin their top edge. On phones, they are bottom sheets with the primary button in
  thumb reach.
- Why: page colour showed through glass, and blur doesn't nest, so a panel opened from the
  backdrop-blurred header or dock let staff lines run through its text.
- `apps/web/DESIGN.md` and `design/README.md` state the new rule.

**What changed:**

- **Foundation (`packages/ui`):**
    - Elevation tokens: `--shadow-raised` and `--shadow-overlay`.
    - Control-height and duration tokens, `--color-record`, error-soft and selected tones, and control and
      tile fills.
    - A shared press/hover/disabled recipe for buttons.
    - `FieldFrame`, `SelectedMark` (one "picked" recipe: primary-soft plus a check), `SuccessMark`,
      `Skeleton` and `PriceTag`.
    - Type roles, icon stroke compensation, and text-wrap balance and pretty.
    - A unified `ErrorScreen` with a "missing" tone.
    - Font-variable loading is fixed, including Newsreader's optical-size axis.
- **Score canvas:** a seamless selection band, a playhead-style cursor, bar controls anchored to the last
  row's ink, a flash on transposed ranges, and notes fading in as they land. Exports stay monochrome.
- **Web primitives:** the overlay shadow on every floating surface. Popovers enter from their anchor,
  sheets animate out, and there is one `PopoverHeader`. Open triggers turn grey, never cyan. Also:
  `Tooltip` with `Keycap`, `HeaderMenu` cards on phones, `Container`, and the chrome footer.
- **Recording moment:** a stable two-line recording strip, hollow count-in dots, a centred stop glyph and
  a bar flash after the take. Plus the limit-dialog callout, inline confirms, a progress row in the
  recordings list, an editor skeleton and missing-score states.
- **Editor:**
    - The dock units are a uniform 46px and the note glyphs are no longer clipped.
    - The header has three groups and a history well.
    - On phones, the dock rows share edges, the transport is centred and record is 56px.
    - The More sheet folds below the selected note.
    - Transpose and key grids no longer leave orphan cells.
    - The first-melody card sits under the first bar.
- **Marketing:**
    - One page grid and section headings.
    - Product tiles, the landing hero composition and the haze without a seam.
    - Pricing presentation, with the wide Arranger card.
    - The shared-score toolbar at one height, and the public status pages.
- **App funnel:** the library (labelled date columns on phones), the settings frame, quiet form errors,
  the auth demo sheet and the onboarding preview.
